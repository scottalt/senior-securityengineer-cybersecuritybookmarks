# 📄 Cybersecurity Resume Templates

> Two free resume templates I built to help people land cybersecurity roles. One for breaking in with no security experience, one for people who already have work history. Both are outcome-driven, ATS-friendly, and one page.

## ⬇️ Download

**Get both templates here → [scottaltiparmak.com/resume-templates](https://scottaltiparmak.com/resume-templates)**

---

## 🟢 No Experience (Entry Level)

For breaking into cybersecurity with little or no paid experience. It leads with proof.

- Projects, home labs, and CTFs sit at the top, because that is what hiring managers look for first.
- Every bullet is written to show a result, not just a task.
- Certifications are placed high, where the ATS and the recruiter both see them fast.
- Consolidated hands-on section so your labs, projects, and competitions read as one strong body of work.

## 🔵 Work Experience

For people who already have IT or security roles behind them.

- Every bullet ties back to a security outcome, even general IT work.
- Includes a mapping of common IT tasks to the security domains, so you can reframe honestly (for example, account lockouts become identity and access management).
- Results-first bullets with a metric in as many as possible.
- Clean, one-page, ATS-safe layout.

---

## ✍️ The one rule that matters most

Write every bullet as: accomplished [X] (the result), measured by [Y] (a number), by doing [Z] (the action and tools). Lead with the outcome. Put a number in wherever you honestly can.
