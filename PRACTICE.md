# 🕹️ Practice Hacking (Legally)

> Reading about security only gets you so far. These are the best places to actually do it, hands-on, in a lab that is built to be attacked. All free to start. Study real attacks in [LEARN.md](LEARN.md), then come here and run them yourself.

> ⚠️ **Only hack what you are given permission to hack.** These platforms are legal because the targets are theirs. Never point these skills at systems you do not own or have written permission to test.

---

## 🎯 Where to practice

| Platform | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **[TryHackMe](https://tryhackme.com)** | Guided rooms that take you from zero to job-ready. Best starting point. |
| **[Hack The Box](https://www.hackthebox.com)** | Realistic machines to break into once you want a real challenge. |
| **[PortSwigger Web Security Academy](https://portswigger.net/web-security)** | The free web-hacking course from the makers of Burp Suite. |
| **[OverTheWire](https://overthewire.org/wargames/)** | Classic Linux and privilege-escalation wargames over SSH. |
| **[pwn.college](https://pwn.college/)** | Free university-grade offensive security, from Arizona State. |
| **[picoCTF](https://picoctf.org/)** | Beginner-friendly capture-the-flag challenges you can start today. |
| **[Blue Team Labs Online](https://blueteamlabs.online/)** | The defensive side: investigations, DFIR, and SOC-style challenges. |

## 🧭 How to actually get good

Doing beats watching. Follow this loop.

1. **Start on [TryHackMe](https://tryhackme.com)** and finish a beginner path before jumping to harder platforms.
2. **Write up every box or challenge you complete.** Explaining it is how it sticks, and write-ups double as portfolio proof for your resume.
3. **Move to [Hack The Box](https://www.hackthebox.com) and [pwn.college](https://pwn.college/)** once the basics feel easy.
4. **Try the blue side too.** Attack and defense teach each other, and [Blue Team Labs Online](https://blueteamlabs.online/) shows you what your attacks look like from the SOC.

> 💡 Bonus: [CyberDefenders](https://cyberdefenders.org) is another excellent free platform for blue-team and DFIR practice.
